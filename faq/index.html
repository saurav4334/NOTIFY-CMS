Frequently Asked Questions

Bulk SMS in Bangladesh — pricing, coverage, sender IDs and integration.

General

Bulk SMS is the sending of a single message to a large list of recipients at once. Businesses use it for promotional campaigns, transactional alerts such as order and payment confirmations, and one-time passwords (OTP). It is widely used across e-commerce, banking, healthcare, education and the NGO sector.

We support the major mobile operators in Bangladesh: Grameenphone, Robi, Banglalink and Teletalk.

With masking SMS, your approved brand name appears as the sender ID. With non-masking SMS, a numeric sender ID is shown instead. Masking costs more per message but makes the sender immediately recognisable to the recipient.
