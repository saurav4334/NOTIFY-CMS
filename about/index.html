Who we are

Bulk SMS, without the guesswork

Notify provides bulk SMS services to businesses in Bangladesh — from small companies sending their first campaign to organisations sending high volumes every month.

We cover promotional and transactional messaging, masking and non-masking sender IDs, OTP delivery and Bangla Unicode SMS, with real-time delivery reporting and REST API integration. Our published rates include VAT, TAX and Dipping, so the figure you see is the figure you pay.
